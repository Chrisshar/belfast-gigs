A music charity and hub in a converted warehouse on Gordon Street in the Cathedral Quarter, set up in 2007 to support Northern Ireland's music scene. Its main room hosts gigs and all-ages shows from up-and-coming local acts, and the building also has rehearsal rooms and an exhibition on the history of music in Northern Ireland.

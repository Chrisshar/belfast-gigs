A Sailortown pub on Dock Street, near the docks in north Belfast, that has traded as the American Bar since the 19th century. It's a sister bar to the Sunflower, with good beer and live music most nights. Upstairs, the Dock Street Jazz Club, curated by drummer David Lyttle, brings Irish and international jazz players to a small room every Sunday.

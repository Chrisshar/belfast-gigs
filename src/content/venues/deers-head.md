A 19th-century pub on Lower Garfield Street, just off Royal Avenue, with its own brewery and trad sessions in the bar. The Music Hall upstairs is a 200-capacity room for blues, rock, folk and indie, from local acts to touring bands from Ireland, the UK and further afield.

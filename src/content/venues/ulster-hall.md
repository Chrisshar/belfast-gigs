Belfast's grand Victorian concert hall on Bedford Street, opened in 1862 and home to the Mulholland Grand Organ. Led Zeppelin played Stairway to Heaven live for the first time here, in 1971. Today it holds about 1,000 for gigs, with standing on the floor and seats in the balcony.

Northern Ireland's biggest indoor venue, at the Odyssey complex on Queen's Quay in the Titanic Quarter. Known as the SSE Arena until July 2026, it holds about 11,000 and is where arena tours stop in Belfast. It's also home to the Belfast Giants ice hockey team.

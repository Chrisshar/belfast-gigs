A not-for-profit arts venue on cobbled Hill Street in the Cathedral Quarter, open since 2006 in a listed building. The main room holds about 240 and puts on folk, jazz, trad, world music and experimental gigs alongside comedy, theatre and club nights. It's a regular home for the Cathedral Quarter Arts Festival.

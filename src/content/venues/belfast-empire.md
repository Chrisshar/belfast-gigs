The Empire on Botanic Avenue started life as a Victorian church, and became the Belfast Empire Music Hall in 1987. Downstairs is a busy pub; upstairs, the music hall is a standing room with a balcony that hosts touring bands, local acts, tribute shows and comedy. It's a short walk from Queen's and the Botanic Gardens.
